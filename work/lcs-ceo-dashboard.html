Case study · Le Coffee Shop, Floreal

A sales dashboard the owner actually opens every day

Built phone-first for a family-run café. It pulls the day's sales from the till system automatically and answers the questions an owner asks at a glance.

  • Today so far against a usual day of the same weekday, updated through the day
  • Yesterday, month-to-date and an end-of-month forecast
  • Hour-by-hour sales and foot traffic from the door counter, plus best sellers
  • Ask Claude: plain-English questions about the sales, answered with the key figures
⚠Sample data only. This demo runs on generated sales, and live features like Ask Claude are switched off. Try the tabs along the bottom of the phone.
